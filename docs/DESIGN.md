# Copperlight Realism

Current approved direction, October 3: **handcrafted miniature realism**. The user approved [copperlight-concept.png](design/copperlight-concept.png), replacing the former orange-glass requirements. Preserve the working implementation and existing local controls while changing its presentation.

- Cream plaster reading surfaces, terracotta ceramic accents, sage navigation, turquoise enamel actions and selections.
- Fine, restrained surface grain; fitted borders, gentle shadows and warm afternoon light. Keep text crisp and message surfaces matte.
- Functional navigation rail, contact list, central conversation and right selected-contact/station sidebar. Sidebar identity changes with the active contact.
- Round contact portraits, distinct contact/station profiles, bounded incoming/outgoing message bubbles and a compact composer.
- Original panoramic miniature valley artwork above the conversation. Artwork contains no controls or text.
- Afternoon is the new-visitor/reset default; saved Night/Afternoon/High contrast choices remain. Night uses cool sage surfaces; High contrast is flat and omits scenery.
- At smaller widths the sidebar moves below the workspace; on phones the rail becomes horizontal, contacts/chat stack and station details follow. Preserve all existing controls and composer visibility.

The render's call/video/media and OS controls are omitted because those capabilities do not exist. Existing contact identities and nine-tailed DSI mascot remain. Messages/drafts, profiles and contact organization keep their existing persistence scopes. Local messaging and preview connection limits remain explicit. Review the implementation screenshots under VISUAL_WORKFLOW.md; a generated mockup is not a working interface.
